## Plans
`plans/` is used for plans.
Each plan gets its own directory with a `README.md`
When finished implementing a plan, ask if I would like to update "Status: " with "Completed".
BUT DO NOT CHANGE IT WITHOUT ASKING.
`CONCEPT.md` is the most important file, weight it heavily.
If two files have two version suffixes: (eg, v2, v3, ...), always consider the highest version to be canonical.
**NEVER** touch `CONCEPT.md`

### Planning

interview me, asking ONE question at a time, to understand what the requirements are.

Also read any other files in the plan directory.
